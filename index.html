JARVIS Social

JARVIS Social is a private, non-commercial tool. Its operator uses it to publish his own videos on his own channels Orbit Workshop and Odd Little Shapes on YouTube and Instagram. The tool is not offered to anyone else.

Use of Google user data

The tool signs in only with the operator's own Google account and requests these YouTube Data API scopes:

  • youtube.upload to upload the operator's videos,
  • youtube.readonly to confirm which channel is connected and to read back the status of an upload,
  • youtube.force-ssl only if the operator enables deleting his own uploads.

Details are in the privacy policy.
